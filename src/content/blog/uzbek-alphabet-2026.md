The Uzbek Latin alphabet changed on October 21, 2026. The reform replaces four letter forms of the 1995 spelling, which used apostrophe marks and two-letter digraphs, with single letters that carry diacritics. This post lists the four replacements and shows an example of the difference.

## What changed

The reformed alphabet was finalized on **October 21, 2026**. It replaces these forms:

| Cyrillic | Legacy 1995 Latin | Reformed Latin (2026) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ў ў** | Oʻ oʻ | **Ö ö** |
| **Ғ ғ** | Gʻ gʻ | **Ğ ğ** |
| **Ш ш** | Sh sh | **Ş ş** |
| **Ч ч** | Ch ch | **Ç ç** |

![Table of the four replacements: Oʻ becomes Ö, Gʻ becomes Ğ, Sh becomes Ş, and Ch becomes Ç](/images/blog/uzbek-alphabet-2026/letter-replacements.svg)

The letters J, Q, H and X are unchanged. The tutuq belgisi (the apostrophe in words such as *ma'lumot*, *e'lon* and *ta'lim*) is not replaced.

## Example

```text
1995 spelling:
"O'zbekiston Respublikasi — go'zal va boy yurt. Biz o'zbek tilida gaplashamiz."

Reformed spelling (from October 21, 2026):
"Özbekiston Respublikasi — gözal va boy yurt. Biz özbek tilida gaplaşamiz."
```
